What it does

Five steps,
every meeting.

From a diarized transcript to one thing to practice.

  1. 1 · DetectFinds each customer objection and files it under one of six types: price, spouse/partner, think about it, competitor, timing, trust. It also captures the rep’s reply.
  2. 2 · SplitRanks reps with at least three meetings by close rate. The top 20% ({{ winmap.top_rep_ids|length }} of {{ winmap.rep_count }} here) form the top cohort.
  3. 3 · CompareFor each objection: how often it comes up and the close rate after it, top vs rest, with a 95% Wilson/Newcombe interval. Gaps under five meetings are hidden.
  4. 4 · CitePicks the best response from a won top-cohort meeting, quoted word for word and linked to its timestamp.
  5. 5 · BriefGives each rep one focus: the objection where their gap times its frequency is largest, plus a practice line and a 30-second drill.
